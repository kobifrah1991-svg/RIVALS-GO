Movement first

Every surface is a route

Fights are won by getting somewhere first. Momentum carries through slides and jumps, and the arena is built in layers you can reach in seconds.

  • SlideCrouch at a run to slide 45% faster for 0.7 s.
  • ClimbJump into any ledge up to 2.4 m and pull yourself up.
  • Jump padsSix pads launch you about 6 m, onto towers and catwalks.
  • StancesCrouch or go prone for a smaller hitbox and a steadier aim.

Twelve fighters

Soldiers and civilians, side by side

Four soldiers, four civilian women and four civilian men, each a fully animated character with a real two-handed grip: trigger finger on the trigger, support hand on the handguard or pump.

  • SoldiersMarcus, Diego, Sarah, Gabriela
  • CiviliansMei, Noa, Shira, Emma, Andre, Leo, Eli, Daniel
  • WeaponsAssault rifle, pump shotgun, bolt-action sniper, pistol, machete

The studio

Created by Kobi Yifrach
